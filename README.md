## 🏆 Featured Engineering Projects

### 🌊 [THALSA — Ocean Knowledge & Exam Telemetry Engine](https://github.com/shubhamkerure07/thalsa)
* Full-stack student learning platform featuring an ocean-depth answer engine calibrated for 2–10 mark technical questions.
* Integrated with KaTeX mathematical equation rendering and real-time telemetry analytics.
* **[Live App](https://thalsa.vercel.app/)** &bull; **[GitHub Repo](https://github.com/shubhamkerure07/thalsa)**

### 🔧 [MechMate — AI Automotive Diagnostic Assistant](https://github.com/shubhamkerure07/mechmate)
* Intelligent diagnostic copilot built for mechanics, car enthusiasts, and vehicle owners.
* Powered by Google Gemini AI to analyze vehicle symptoms, decode OBD-II fault codes, and generate actionable repair instructions.
* **[Live App](https://mechmate-sigma.vercel.app/)** &bull; **[GitHub Repo](https://github.com/shubhamkerure07/mechmate)**

### 🪐 [ORBITAL — Deep Space Simulation & Astrodynamics Lab](https://github.com/shubhamkerure07/orbital)
* High-precision aerospace mission-control console and digital planetarium built with React 18, Three.js, and TypeScript.
* Powered by an analytical SI astrophysical engine and an energy-conserving Symplectic Velocity Verlet gravitational integrator.
* Features near-Earth asteroid tracking (34,850+ asteroids) and exoplanet transit spectroscopy.
* **[Live App](https://orbital-nu-gules.vercel.app/)** &bull; **[GitHub Repo](https://github.com/shubhamkerure07/orbital)**

### 📱 [Native Android Systems](https://github.com/shubhamkerure07)
* **[EduClass (Edu Made Easy)](https://github.com/shubhamkerure07/edu-made-easy)** — Native Android tuition & interactive classroom platform with real-time class notifications and quiz management.
* **[StudentHub](https://github.com/shubhamkerure07/student-manager)** — Android academic planner with auto-alarms, attendance margin calculations, and Canvas expense tracking.
* **[MITE Contineo Enhanced](https://github.com/shubhamkerure07/mite-contineo-webs-enhanced-version)** — Modernized campus academic portal redesign with attendance margin predictors and AI integration.
